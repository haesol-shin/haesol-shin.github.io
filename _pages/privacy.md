Haesol Personal Assistant is a personal-use assistant operated by and for the owner of this website only. It is not offered to other users.

**Data accessed.** With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the assistant reads and writes the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks data.

**Use.** The data is used only to provide the assistant's functions to the owner, such as schedule and mail summaries, reminders and task management.

**Storage and sharing.** The data is processed on a server controlled by the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

**Revocation.** Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

**Contact.** Use the contact links on the [home page](/).
